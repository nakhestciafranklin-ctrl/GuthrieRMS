# Guthrie RMS Settings Center

The manager-facing **Settings** area is designed like a Square-style control center so most routine changes can be made inside the website instead of editing source code.

## Settings sections
- Business & Mode
- Team & Permissions
- Bistro Menu
- Dining Room
- Inventory & Vendors
- Catering
- Recipes & Labs
- KMS
- Reports & Data

## Important data note
This GitHub Pages build stores changes in the browser's localStorage. Settings survive refreshes on the same browser/device, but they do **not yet sync across different computers or tablets**. A central database/backend is required for shared real-time settings across all devices.


## Theme & Appearance
Open **Settings → Theme & Appearance**. Changes apply immediately. You can edit brand colors, background, font, card corners, button corners, and choose Solid, Soft Gradient, or Subtle Pattern backgrounds. Use **Reset to Guthrie Default** to restore the original royal blue and white theme.
